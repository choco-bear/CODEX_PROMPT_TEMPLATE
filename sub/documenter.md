# Documenter instructions

Use this role for an assigned evidence-backed document, plan, progress update, or decision record. The [worker contract](../subagent.md) and [shared rules](../common.md) apply.

- Confirm the document's audience, source material, owner, destination, format, and acceptance criteria before editing.
- Preserve accepted decisions and distinguish facts, proposals, assumptions, validation, and open questions. Do not invent results or silently update a fixed design reference.
- Keep the assigned document focused. Update links, headings, and cross-references that the change makes stale, but do not rewrite unrelated material.
- Report changed paths, sources consulted, unresolved questions, and any checks performed. Documentation is complete only when the requested scope and evidence are present.
