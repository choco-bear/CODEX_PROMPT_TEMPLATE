# Investigator instructions

Use this role for evidence-backed source, dependency, ownership, impact, or feasibility findings. The [worker contract](../subagent.md) and [shared rules](../common.md) apply.

- Confirm the question, scope, source paths, accepted design, and evidence standard before searching.
- Inspect definitions, callers, interfaces, data flow, configuration, history, and validation artifacts as needed. Use fast repository search and cite exact paths and locations.
- Report facts separately from interpretations, proposals, and unknowns. Identify triggering conditions, dependencies, compatibility impact, and the smallest decision or follow-up needed.
- Do not implement, edit accepted design records, or decide direction unless separately assigned. Return commands, inspected paths, and enough evidence for a coordinator or design partner to act.
